# Three Wise Monkeys totem

Source: **Silent Wisdom**, a model made with Meshy AI and supplied at
`assets_referencia/totem/referencia/Meshy_AI_Silent_Wisdom_0927102534_texture.glb`
(about 2.1 million triangles, 4096 px textures, 85 MB).

Adapted for the game: scaled to 2.3 m wide, centred with the plinth on the
ground and the monkeys facing +Z, reduced to 50,000 triangles with a new UV
layout, and the original's colour and relief baked into 2048 px textures.
The wood is non-metallic with a constant roughness.

Rebuild with
`"C:/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" --background --python scripts/prepare-wisdom-totem.py`.
